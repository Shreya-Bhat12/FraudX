Decision Thresholds
🚫 BLOCK — score ≥ 0.80
Decline transaction. Freeze account. Notify fraud team immediately.
⚠️ ALERT — score 0.55–0.80
Send OTP verification. Flag for manual review. Hold transaction.
✅ ALLOW — score < 0.55
Transaction approved. Continue passive monitoring.
